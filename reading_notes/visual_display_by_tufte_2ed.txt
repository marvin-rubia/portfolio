Ch1: Graphical Excellence
• Graphical displays should induce the viewer to think about the substance rather than about methodology, design, etc., and should encourage them to compare different pieces of data at different levels (broad and granular), and graphical displays should be closely integrated with statistical and verbal descriptions of a dataset.
• A visualization can easily show the few outliers in the data.
